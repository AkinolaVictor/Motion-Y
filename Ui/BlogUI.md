Design the **Writing page** for Akinola Victor's AI Engineer portfolio. Maintain the exact visual language established on the Home, Projects, About, and Lab pages: **dark-first, premium, minimal, technical, sophisticated, futuristic but professional**. Use a near-black/charcoal background, soft-white typography, muted gray secondary text, and one consistent electric-blue/cyan accent. Use modern sans-serif typography with subtle monospace technical metadata. Avoid excessive gradients, glassmorphism, neon effects, stock imagery, or generic blog templates.

## PAGE PURPOSE

This is Akinola's technical **blog and learning journal** — a place to document things he is learning, building, experimenting with, and discovering as an AI Engineer.

The page should communicate:

> **“I learn by building, experimenting, and sharing what I discover.”**

Prioritize readability and strong editorial design over decorative effects.

## HERO

Title:

> **Writing**

Supporting text:

> Notes on AI engineering, software development, experiments, and lessons learned while building intelligent systems.

Add subtle technical metadata such as:

**ARTICLES · LEARNING · ENGINEERING · EXPERIMENTS**

## CATEGORIES

Create clear category filters:

**All · AI Engineering · AI + Development · Technical Experiments**

Allow visitors to quickly filter articles without leaving the page.

## FEATURED ARTICLE

Include a prominent featured article at the top with:

- Large visual/technical preview
- Title
- Short excerpt
- Category
- Publication date
- Reading time
- **Read Article →**

Use an editorial layout rather than a conventional card.

## ARTICLE LIST

Display articles in a clean, highly readable list/grid.

Example content:

### AI Engineering

- Understanding Embeddings
- Building an Agent From Scratch

### AI + Development
- How I Use Claude Code in Development
- Building an AI-Native Development Workflow

### Technical Experiments

Each article should contain:

- Title
- Short excerpt
- Category

## ARTICLE DETAIL PREVIEW

Design the system so each article can later have its own dedicated page with:

**Title → Metadata → Introduction → Content → Code/Diagrams → Key Takeaways → Related Articles**

Support syntax-highlighted code, technical diagrams, images, tables, and citations.

## CONSISTENCY

Reuse the exact navbar, footer, typography, colors, spacing, buttons, badges, borders, accent color, and responsive behavior established throughout the portfolio.

Use subtle purposeful animations and micro-interactions, but keep article content highly readable.

The page should feel like a **premium technical publication combined with a personal engineering journal**.

Overall impression:

> **“This engineer doesn't just build things — he understands, experiments, and shares what he learns.”**
