Design the **“Victor's AI Lab” page** for Akinola Victor's AI Engineer portfolio. Maintain the exact visual language established on the Home, Projects, and About pages: **dark-first, premium, minimal, technical, sophisticated, futuristic but professional**. Use a near-black/charcoal background, soft-white typography, muted gray secondary text, and one consistent electric-blue/cyan accent. ## PAGE PURPOSE

**Victor's AI Lab** is an experimental playground demonstrating active learning, research, experimentation, and curiosity.

It should explicitly feel different from the polished Projects page. Projects represent **engineered showcases**; the Lab represents **experiments, discoveries, failures, and learning**.

The page should communicate:

> **“I'm not only building AI systems — I'm actively exploring how they work.”**

Do not present experiments as production products or fake professional experience.

## HERO

Title:

> **Victor's AI Lab**

Supporting text:

Add subtle metadata such as:

**EXPERIMENTS · RESEARCH · LEARNING · PROTOTYPES**

Each experiment should contain:

**Question**  
What am I trying to understand or solve?

**Experiment**  
What approach, model, architecture, or technique was tested?

**Result**  
What happened? Include metrics or observations where relevant.

**What I Learned**  
The key insight or lesson discovered.

Also display:

- Experiment category
- Date
- Technologies/models used
- Status such as **Exploring, Successful, Failed, Iterating**
- Optional GitHub link

Use experimental visual treatments such as notebook-like metadata, numbered experiments, terminal-inspired elements, subtle diagrams, and technical annotations.
