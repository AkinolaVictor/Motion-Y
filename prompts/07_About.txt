6. ACCESSIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Each <section> has an aria-label or aria-labelledby.
- The four social links in Social.jsx are real <a target=
  "_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> with visible text
  labels (not icon-only).
- The "View résumé" and "Download CV" buttons are real
  anchors; when disabled, they keep their href but add
  aria-disabled="true" and a tooltip.
- The "Build → Measure → Improve" statement uses real text,
  not images; screen readers read "Build Measure Improve".

----------------------------------------------------------------
7. DO NOT
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Do NOT turn this page into a résumé. It is a personal
  statement, not a CV.
- Do NOT add a downloadable vCard.
- Do NOT add a "Hire me" CTA — that lives on /contact.
- Do NOT add a profile photo carousel; one portrait, used
  in AboutHero and Social, is enough.
- Do NOT add a "currently reading" section.
- Do NOT change the three social URLs (GitHub, LinkedIn, X)
  or the email address — they are the canonical links.

----------------------------------------------------------------
8. ASSUMPTIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------
- The "AI Engineer focused on building intelligent
  applications" line is the canonical sub-headline and is
  used here, on the home page, and in meta tags. It is the
  single-sentence identity statement.
- The four links in the social block are exactly: GitHub,
  LinkedIn, X. Email is rendered separately as a mono
  "or just email: " link, not as a fourth icon.
- "Build → Measure → Improve" is the canonical engineering
  philosophy. The arrows are unicode U+2192, not ascii "->".
- The Learning list (5 items) is a snapshot, not a feed.
  The page does not auto-update from anywhere; the data file
  is updated by hand.
- The timeline starts at 2019 and ends at "now" (2026).
  Intermediate years are inferred from the data.
- The résumé PDF is at /public/resume.pdf. If absent, the
  buttons render disabled but the page does not break.
