We collect and process information for legitimate business purposes, including:

1. **Business Assessment:** To understand your company's structure, operations, workflows and challenges.

2. **AI Opportunity Identification:** To identify processes that could benefit from artificial intelligence, automation, intelligent software and system integrations.

3. **Personalized Recommendations:** To develop relevant recommendations and potential solutions based on your business requirements.

4. **Communication:** To contact you with assessment findings, recommendations, updates and responses to your enquiries.

5. **Consultation and Implementation:** To arrange consultations, discuss potential solutions and, where requested and agreed, implement AI and automation systems.

6. **Service Improvement:** To improve our services, assessment processes and business operations.

7. **Legal Compliance:** To meet applicable legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

We will not use your information for purposes that are incompatible with the purposes described in this policy without an appropriate lawful basis.

## 4. AI-Assisted Analysis and Processing

As an AI Systems and Business Automation company, Motion-Y may use artificial intelligence and automated technologies to assist with analysing business assessment responses and identifying potential opportunities.

This may include:
- Analysing business workflows and operational structures.
- Identifying repetitive tasks and inefficiencies.
- Recognizing patterns in business processes.

- Generating preliminary recommendations and reports.

AI-generated findings may be reviewed and refined by our team before recommendations are communicated.

AI-assisted analysis does not automatically mean that a final business decision or implementation recommendation has been made. We do not intend to make decisions about individuals solely through automated processing where such decisions would have legal or similarly significant effects.
