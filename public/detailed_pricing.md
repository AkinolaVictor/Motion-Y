- 60% upfront.
- 40% upon completion.

- 50% upfront.
- 30% at the agreed project milestone.
- 20% upon delivery.

**Large Projects**
- Milestone-based payments.

- 100% payment before the consultation session.

**Monthly Services**
- Payment in advance.

## 6. General Commercial Terms

1. Project development begins after receipt of the required initial payment.
2. Project requirements and deliverables must be agreed upon before development.
3. Standard project packages include up to two revision rounds.
4. Additional features or requirements outside the agreed scope attract additional charges.
5. Final project handover follows settlement of outstanding payments.
6. Third-party expenses are billed separately unless explicitly included in the proposal.
7. Applicable taxes are charged separately.
8. All prices are starting prices and may be adjusted based on project complexity.
9. Custom quotations are provided for projects outside the standard packages.
10. Project timelines are agreed upon individually.

## 7. Currency & Exchange Rate Policy

Motion-Y supports pricing in Nigerian Naira (NGN) and US Dollars (USD).

- Nigerian clients are generally quoted in NGN.
- International clients are generally quoted in USD.
- USD prices are the primary reference prices for international quotations.
- NGN prices are indicative local equivalents.
- Naira pricing may be reviewed periodically to reflect exchange rate changes.

**Reference exchange rate used for this pricing structure:**
- $1 = ₦1,347.01
- Reference date: November 9, 2026.

Exchange rates are subject to change and should be verified before issuing quotations.
