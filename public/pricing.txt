# Motion-Y — Service Pricing Structure

**Company:** Motion-Y  
**Category:** AI Agency  
**Positioning:** AI Systems & Business Automation  
**Target Market:** Nigeria and International  
**Pricing Model:** Mid-range / Professional  
**Currencies:** NGN and USD

All prices are starting prices. Final quotations depend on project scope, complexity and technical requirements.

## 1. Process Automation
Automating repetitive business operations, workflows and administrative processes.

- **Starter:** $350 / ₦475,000 — 1 simple workflow, up to 2 integrations.
- **Professional:** $900 / ₦1,210,000 — 3–5 workflows, up to 5 integrations.
- **Advanced:** $2,000+ / ₦2,695,000+ — 6–10 workflows, complex logic and integrations.

## 2. AI Agents
Developing intelligent agents that interact with tools, applications and business systems.

- **Starter:** $900 / ₦1,210,000 — Single-purpose agent, 1–2 tools.
- **Professional:** $2,500 / ₦3,370,000 — Advanced agent, 3–5 tools and multi-step execution.
- **Advanced:** $7,500+ / ₦10,100,000+ — Multiple agents, orchestration and complex integrations.

## 3. API & Integrations
Connecting applications, databases, platforms and third-party services.

- **Basic:** $250 / ₦337,000 — 1 API integration.
- **Standard:** $750 / ₦1,010,000 — 2–4 integrations.
- **Advanced:** $1,800+ / ₦2,425,000+ — 5+ integrations and custom middleware.

## 4. AI Consultancy
Helping businesses identify AI opportunities and develop implementation strategies.

- **Consultation:** $100 / ₦135,000 — 60-minute session.
- **AI Assessment:** $350 / ₦475,000 — Business process assessment and AI opportunity report.
- **AI Strategy:** $750 / ₦1,010,000 — Detailed AI adoption roadmap and implementation plan.

## 5. Intelligent Software
Developing custom software applications with AI capabilities and intelligent functionality.

- **MVP:** $1,500 / ₦2,020,000 — Basic functional application.
- **Professional:** $4,000 / ₦5,390,000 — Full application with backend, database and integrations.
- **Advanced:** $9,000+ / ₦12,120,000+ — Complex AI-powered software with multiple modules.

## 6. Monthly Maintenance & Managed Services

- **Basic:** $75 / ₦101,000 — Monitoring, minor fixes and basic support.
- **Professional:** $200 / ₦269,000 — Maintenance, updates, optimisation and technical support.
- **Premium:** $450 / ₦606,000 — Priority support, monitoring and ongoing improvements.

## 7. Commercial Terms

- Small projects: 60% upfront, 40% on completion.
- Medium projects: 50% upfront, 30% at milestone, 20% on delivery.
- Large projects: Milestone-based payments.
- Consultancy: 100% upfront.
- Monthly services: Paid in advance.
- Two revision rounds included in standard projects.
- Third-party subscriptions, hosting, API usage and applicable taxes are charged separately unless agreed otherwise.
- Custom quotations apply to projects outside standard packages.

## 8. Currency Policy

Nigerian clients are generally quoted in NGN and international clients in USD. Naira equivalents are indicative and subject to exchange-rate reviews.

**Reference exchange rate:** $1 = ₦1,347.01 (November 9, 2026).
