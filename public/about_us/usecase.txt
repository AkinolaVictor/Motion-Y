How Motion-Y Works

Motion-Y generally approaches AI projects through several stages:

Understand the business problem.
Identify workflows and opportunities where AI can provide value.
Define the required solution and technical architecture.
Build and integrate the AI system.
Test the system against realistic use cases.
Deploy the solution.
Monitor, improve, and expand the system when necessary.
